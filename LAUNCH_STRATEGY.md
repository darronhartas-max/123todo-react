# 123ToDo — Public Launch & Promotion Strategy Guide

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Positioning

**123ToDo** ([app.123todo.com](https://app.123todo.com)) is positioned as a **100% free, zero-knowledge encrypted, dual-skin productivity PWA** that offers features commonly paywalled in mainstream apps (Todoist, TickTick, Any.do, Google Keep).

---

## 🔑 Core Value Hooks

1. **100% Free & Unlimited**: No paywalled recurring tasks, subtasks, priority matrices, or project limits.
2. **Zero-Knowledge Privacy (Client-Side AES-256-GCM)**: Direct sync to user's Google Drive or Cloudflare D1 with client-side encryption.
3. **Dual-Skin OS**: Instant 1-tap toggle between structured Task Manager Mode (P1–P4 matrix) and Simple Voice Notes / Unassigned Inbox Mode.
4. **Universal Migration Wizard**: 1-click import from Todoist, TickTick, Google Keep, Google Tasks, and Microsoft To Do.
5. **No App Store Friction**: Fast, lightweight PWA running seamlessly on mobile, desktop, and tablet.

## 🚀 Zero-Budget Organic Launch Blueprint

### 2. Community & Developer Seeding

- **Hacker News (Show HN)**:
  - _Title_: `Show HN: 123ToDo – A private, free PWA task manager with E2E encrypted Google Drive sync`
  - _Focus_: React 19, PWA architecture, AES-256-GCM client-side encryption, zero telemetry.
- **Reddit**:
  - `r/productivity`: Authentic solo maker story on why you built a free alternative to subscription bloat.
  - `r/todoist`: Highlight the 1-click migration wizard and free feature parity.
  - `r/SideProject` & `r/webdev`: Share technical and Antigravity workflow insights.
  - `r/privacy` & `r/selfhosted`: Emphasize client-side encryption and user-owned Google Drive storage.

### 3. SEO & Long-Tail Organic Discovery

- Publish landing comparison pages (e.g. `123todo.com/todoist-alternative`) highlighting side-by-side pricing, privacy, and feature differences.

## 🎯 What is the 1-2-3 Rule?

The **1-2-3 Rule** is a simple yet transformative time-management framework designed to combat cognitive overload:

- **1 Must-Do Task (P1)**: The single highest-leverage, non-negotiable objective of your day. Completing this ensures your day is a definitive win.
- **2 Should-Do Tasks (P2)**: Important, high-value tasks that move major projects and goals forward.
- **3 Could-Do Tasks (P3)**: Quick wins, errands, or low-friction follow-ups that you can tackle if time and energy permit.

By constraining your daily focus to 6 carefully prioritized tasks, you eliminate the paralyzing effect of 50-item to-do lists and finish each day with clarity and satisfaction.

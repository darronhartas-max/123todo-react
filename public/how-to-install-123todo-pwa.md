# What is a PWA & How to Install 123 To Do on Your Device

*Published by the 123 ToDo Team • Beginner-Friendly Guide*

Have you ever wondered why **123 To Do** feels so fast, clean, and responsive? Or why you don't have to search for it in Apple's App Store or Google Play Store?

The secret is that 123 To Do is built as a modern **Progressive Web App (PWA)**. 

If you've never heard the term "PWA" before, don't worry—most people haven't! In this short guide, we’ll explain what it means in plain English, why you should definitely install it on your device, and exactly how to do it in under 10 seconds.

---

## What on Earth is a "PWA"?

Think of a **Progressive Web App** as the best of both worlds:
- It looks, feels, and works like a **native mobile or desktop app** that you downloaded from an app store.
- But it installs **instantly and directly from your web browser**—with no big downloads, no app store passwords, no tracking, and zero clutter.

When you install 123 To Do on your phone, tablet, or laptop, it gets its own official app icon on your **Home Screen**, **Dock**, or **Start Menu**. When you tap it, it opens instantly in full screen—just like any other app on your device!

---

## Why You Should Install 123 To Do (Instead of Keeping a Browser Tab Open)

Many people first visit 123 To Do in a browser like Safari, Chrome, or Edge and simply leave the tab open. While that works, installing it as an app gives you a vastly better experience:

### 1. 🚀 Instant 1-Tap Access from Your Home Screen or Dock
No more opening your browser, searching through 40 open tabs, or typing in the web address. Simply tap the **123 To Do** icon right on your home screen or dock to jump straight into your tasks.

### 2. 📱 Full-Screen, Distraction-Free Experience
When you install 123 To Do, all the browser clutter disappears. There are no address bars, back/forward buttons, or tab strips eating up your screen space. You get 100% of your screen dedicated to your tasks and notes.

### 3. ✈️ Works 100% Offline (Anywhere, Anytime)
Going into a subway tunnel? Flying on an airplane? In an area with spotty cell reception? 
Because 123 To Do is installed on your device, it loads instantly and lets you add, edit, and check off tasks without an active internet connection. As soon as you reconnect, your encrypted sync picks up right where you left off.

### 4. 🛡️ Better Data Safety & Memory Persistence
Web browsers occasionally clear website storage and cookies if a tab hasn’t been visited for a long time or when storage space runs low. When you install 123 To Do as an app, your operating system treats its database with high priority, keeping your tasks and offline data secure and persistent.

### 5. ⚡ Ultra-Lightweight & Zero Hassle
Unlike regular app store downloads that can take up hundreds of megabytes, 123 To Do takes up practically zero space on your device. Plus, you never have to visit an app store to install updates—it updates automatically in the background whenever a new version is released.

## How to Install 123 To Do (Step-by-Step)

Installing takes just a few seconds. Find your device below and follow the simple steps:

### 📱 iPhone & iPad (Using Safari)
*Apple requires adding PWAs through Safari:*

1. Open **[123todo.com](https://www.123todo.com)** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share button** at the bottom of your screen (the square icon with an arrow pointing up ⬆️).
3. Scroll down the menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"** (look for the `+` icon).
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. **That's it!** You will now see the 123 To Do icon on your home screen. Tap it to launch your new app!

> *Tip: If you opened 123 To Do inside Chrome or another browser on iPhone, tap the share button and choose "Open in Safari" first, then follow the steps above.*

---

### 🤖 Android Phones & Tablets (Using Chrome)

1. Open **[123todo.com](https://www.123todo.com)** in **Google Chrome**.
2. Look for the prompt banner at the top/bottom that says **"Add 123 To Do to Home Screen"** or **"Install"**, and tap it.
3. *If you don't see the banner:* Tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top right corner of Chrome.
4. Tap **"Install app"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
5. Tap **"Install"** to confirm.
6. **Done!** 123 To Do is now in your app drawer and on your home screen.

---

### 💻 Mac, Windows PC & Chromebook (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open **[123todo.com](https://www.123todo.com)** in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Look at the right side of the top address bar (where the web address is).
3. Click the **Install icon** (it looks like a small computer monitor with a down arrow, or a `+` symbol).
4. Click **"Install"**.
5. 123 To Do will open in its own clean window, and you can pin it to your **Taskbar** (Windows) or keep it in your **Dock** (Mac)!

> *Mac Safari Users (macOS Sonoma or newer):* Click **File** in the top menu bar ➔ choose **"Add to Dock"** ➔ click **"Add"**.
